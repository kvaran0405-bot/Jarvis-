CONNECT GOOGLE CALENDAR

About 5 minutes, once. After this, JARVIS sees your real Google Calendar, and anything the agents add shows up on your phone's calendar too.

  1. Copy your JARVIS script. It already contains your own secret key.
    Loading…
  2. Open script.google.com → New project (sign in with the Google account whose calendar you use).
  3. Select all the sample code in the editor (Ctrl+A), delete it, and paste your script (Ctrl+V). Click the 💾 Save icon. Name the project JARVIS if it asks.
  4. At the top, choose the function authorize and click ▶ Run. Google asks for permission: Review permissions → pick your account → Advanced → Go to JARVIS (unsafe) → Allow.
    “Unsafe” only means Google hasn't reviewed it: it's your own script, running in your own account.
  5. Click the blue Deploy button → New deployment → the ⚙ gear → Web app. Set:
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone
    “Anyone” is needed so JARVIS can reach it without a Google login; the secret key stops everyone else.
    Click Deploy, then Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).
